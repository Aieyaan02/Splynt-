Privacy policy

How Splynt handles information when you use our website and inventory pilot.

Last updated: November 6, 2026

Who operates Splynt

Splynt is operated by Aieyaan Yeasin as an individual, not an LLC. For privacy questions or requests, email Aieyaan.yeasin@gmail.com.

This policy covers the Splynt website, store accounts, support inquiries, and the initial inventory integration with Clover. Clover and other service providers have their own privacy policies for their services.

Information we collect

  • Account and workspace information: information you provide when registering or managing your account, organization, and stores, such as your email, name, store details, membership, and settings. Passwords are stored as hashes rather than readable passwords.
  • Connected inventory: merchant identifiers and relevant store properties, product names and identifiers, barcodes, categories, prices, stock quantities, and inventory history needed to display and manage your imported inventory.
  • Connection and service records: POS authorization credentials, connection status, synchronization results, and technical or security records used to operate and protect the service. Hosting infrastructure may also process IP addresses and request information.
  • Support information: your name, email, business details, messages, and any attachments you choose to send. Messages submitted through the website are stored for follow-up.

The initial inventory pilot is not intended to import Clover customer records, employee records, payment information, or sales orders. Do not include customer payment details or other unnecessary sensitive information in support messages.

How we use information

We use information to create and authenticate accounts, control access to workspaces, connect your authorized store, display inventory, calculate inventory-based restock suggestions, diagnose synchronization problems, respond to requests, and prevent misuse.

The initial pilot does not place orders for you or send inventory to an external AI model for forecasting. Future features that materially change data use will require an updated notice before that use begins.

Who can access information

Authorized members of your workspace can access its information according to their permissions. The Splynt operator can access information as needed for service administration, troubleshooting, security, and verified support requests.

We use Render to host the application and Neon to store application data. Connecting a store exchanges authorization and API requests with Clover. Support email is handled through Gmail. These providers process information needed to deliver their services; information may be processed in the United States and other locations where providers operate.

We do not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising. We may disclose information when required by law, to respond to lawful requests, or when reasonably necessary to protect the service and its users.

Browser storage

The Splynt application uses browser session storage for its sign-in token and selected store. This supports your session and workspace navigation. Signing out clears the application’s sign-in token. These support and privacy pages do not load advertising trackers or external analytics scripts.

Retention, disconnection, and deletion

Account and workspace records remain in the service while you use it, unless deleted. Disconnecting a POS connection does not automatically delete previously imported inventory. Uninstalling an integration is also separate from deleting your Splynt account or workspace.

An organization owner can use the account/workspace deletion option to remove the workspace, its stores, imported inventory, history, and saved POS credentials from the active application database. This requires password verification and typed confirmation. Other members’ accounts and unrelated workspaces remain. Delete any workspaces you own before deleting your account. Deleting Splynt records does not delete records held by Clover.

Support messages and technical or security records are handled separately and are not all removed by account deletion. The pilot does not automatically purge all such records on a fixed schedule. Contact us to request review or deletion of those records. Some information may need to be retained for security, resolving disputes, or legal obligations.

Deletion from the active database does not guarantee immediate removal from provider backups or logs. Any retained copies are subject to the provider’s applicable retention and recovery settings. Contact us for information about your deletion request.

Your choices and requests

You can review and update available account or store settings, disconnect a POS connection, and request access, correction, or deletion by emailing Aieyaan.yeasin@gmail.com. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under applicable privacy law.

We may ask for information to verify your identity and authority over the affected account or workspace. Do not send passwords or authentication codes. If your request concerns information a merchant maintains in Clover, contact that merchant or Clover as appropriate.

Security

Splynt uses account authentication, access controls, password hashing, and encrypted storage for saved POS credentials. Access over the public website uses HTTPS. No service can guarantee absolute security. Report suspected unauthorized access to the support email promptly.

Children and policy updates

Splynt is intended for business users and is not directed to children under 13. Contact us if you believe a child has provided personal information so we can investigate.

We will update this page and its date when this policy changes. Where required, we will provide additional notice or obtain consent for material changes.
