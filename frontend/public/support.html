Getting started

  1. Open Splynt and sign in or create your account.
  2. Open your store workspace and choose Connect your POS system.
  3. Authorize the correct merchant account with your provider, then return to Splynt.
  4. Choose Sync now and check the last successful sync before relying on quantities.

Production connections depend on the provider allowing installation of the app and your authority to connect that merchant account.

Common questions

Does Splynt change inventory in my POS?

The initial inventory pilot imports Clover inventory into Splynt. It does not write inventory changes back to Clover, process payments, or place supplier orders. Confirm suggested purchases against your store records.

Why does the page take a while to load?

The pilot uses free hosting that may sleep during inactivity. Allow time for it to wake, then retry. Contact support if the page continues to fail.

Why is my inventory out of date?

The pilot uses manual synchronization. Choose Sync now and review the result. A failed sync does not mean the displayed inventory is current. Unknown stock needs checking in your POS.

What if my POS connection fails?

Check that you authorized the intended merchant account and that your app installation is permitted. Try connecting again from your store workspace. Send support the safe error message if the issue persists.

I forgot my password. What should I do?

Use the password recovery option if email recovery is available. If it says email delivery is unavailable, contact support from your registered email address. We will verify ownership before assisting; do not send your password.

How do I disconnect or delete my data?

Disconnecting your POS stops that connection but does not erase imported records. Use Splynt’s Delete account or workspace option for deletion. Workspace deletion is restricted to its owner and requires your password and typed confirmation. Delete your owned workspaces before deleting your account. This does not delete records from Clover. For inquiries, privacy requests, or trouble accessing your account, email support. Read the privacy policy for details.

Can I use Splynt on my phone?

Open the Splynt website in your phone’s browser. The pilot is a web application, not a native Clover device application.
